About CycleCore

Building AI tools developers can trust.

Our Mission

To build AI infrastructure with privacy as the foundation, not an afterthought.

We create tools that respect developer sovereignty, practice radical transparency, and reject surveillance capitalism.

Too many developer tools prioritize data extraction over user trust. Too many privacy policies are intentionally vague. Too many companies treat developers as products to monetize.

We're building an alternative.

CycleCore Technologies builds AI tools with privacy-first architecture—whether local or cloud. We're transparent about our practices, honest about trade-offs, and committed to earning your trust through action, not promises.

How We're Different

🔒 Privacy by Design

Not bolted on after launch. Built into the architecture from day one. Local tools process everything on your device. Cloud tools collect only essential data, encrypted end-to-end.

🔍 Radically Transparent

We tell you exactly what data goes where, why it's collected, and how it's protected. No vague policies. No hidden tracking. Honest about trade-offs.

💰 No Surveillance Business Model

We make money by building tools worth paying for—not by monetizing your data. No behavioral profiling. No third-party data sales. No advertising.

⚡ Developer Sovereignty

You choose the tools that fit your needs. No vendor lock-in. No forced migrations. No surprise pricing changes. Own your stack, keep your freedom.

📖 Building Trust Through Transparency

We document our practices clearly, communicate honestly about trade-offs, and remain accountable to our users. Trust is earned through consistent action.

Values in Action

Our principles aren't just words on a page. They guide every decision we make:

  • → Product design: Privacy-first architecture, minimal data collection
  • → Communication: Transparent documentation, honest marketing
  • → Business model: Sustainable pricing, no surveillance monetization
  • → Development: Security audits, careful dependency management
  • → Support: Respectful of your time, no dark patterns

Every feature, every line of code, every policy reflects our commitment to privacy, transparency, and trust.
