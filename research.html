A Large-Scale ML-Guided Search for 28-Term Prime Progressions: No Progressions with More Than 10 Primes Found Among 10^9 Candidates

We present a computational search for arithmetic progressions of primes with 28 or more terms. Using ML-guided candidate generation and distributed verification across 10^9 candidates, we find no progressions exceeding 10 primes among the search space.
