Common Questions

Do you use third-party trackers?

No. We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party tracking. Basic product analytics (feature usage) are handled in-house with minimal data collection.

Do you sell or share user data?

Never. We don't sell, rent, or share user data with third parties for marketing or advertising. Your data stays with us or gets deleted—period.

Can I use your tools without an internet connection?

CycleSum Lite: Yes. 100% offline, no internet required.
MCPBodega: No. Cloud-hosted SaaS requires internet connection.

Where is my data stored?

CycleSum Lite: Your device only. We never see your data.
MCPBodega: Enterprise cloud hosting with industry-standard security. All data encrypted at rest and in transit.

How do you make money without selling data?

We charge for the tools themselves. One-time purchases for local tools. Subscriptions for cloud tools. Simple, sustainable, no surveillance.

Can I verify your privacy claims?

Yes. CycleSum Lite processes everything locally, so your data never reaches our servers. For MCPBodega, we provide detailed documentation of our data practices in our privacy policy and on this page.
